# Putting App — Warm-up & Game Drills

Oct 28, 2026 · @Christian

A 15-minute warm-up of two unscored drills, then 15 games. Each game has a fixed setup, produces one number, and carries Tour-anchored targets, so scores compare across sessions, greens and players.

## Rules that make scores comparable

- **Fixed setup:** distances, gate widths and zone sizes are fixed per game. A score only counts on a leaderboard if the setup was exactly as written; any variant is logged as "Hard mode" or "Custom".
- **Slope class:** every game names one. Flat = side slope < 1% (a ball rolled by hand from 1 m tracks straight); Breaking = 1–2.5%; Steep = > 2.5%. Log the class with the score.
- **Green speed:** log Slow (Stimp < 9), Medium (9–11) or Fast (> 11), from the club or your own judgement. Compare scores within the same speed band.
- **Measuring:** from the hole centre (or target tee centre) with a tape. Putter lengths are fine for setup, not for scoring.
- **One attempt per ball, no gimmes, full routine.** Re-putts never count.
- **Targets** are given for three levels: Beginner / mid-handicap (≈ 15–28), Low single digit (≈ 0–5) and Tour. Tour targets are anchored to PGA Tour ShotLink make % and 3-putt rates; the other levels are coaching targets.
- **Diagram legend:** O = hole · • = ball · | = tee (gate post) · T = tee marker · ---- = alignment stick · → = putt direction.

## Warm-up (15 minutes, unscored)

Two stations, run in order before any game. No scoring: the goal is to set up correctly, groove the stroke and read the green speed.

### W1 · Setup & Alignment Station (7 min)

**Equipment:** 1 putter · 3 balls · 1 alignment stick · 3 tees · optional putting mirror.

**Setup:**

- Flat, straight putt of 2.0 m.
- Alignment stick on the ground parallel to the target line, 15 cm beyond the ball (far side).
- Tee at the ball position.
- Start gate: 2 tees 0.3 m in front of the ball, 6.0 cm inner gap, centred on the line to the hole.

**Procedure:**

1. Minutes 0–3 — five setup checks. Walk in with your routine and set up. Then check: eyes over the line (hold a ball at the bridge of your nose and drop it — it should land on or just inside the ball), feet and shoulders parallel to the stick, putter face square to the stick. Step out fully between checks.
2. Minutes 3–7 — 10 putts through the gate with full routine, at the speed that would finish 0.4 m past if missed. Watch the start line only; ignore whether it holes.

**Diagram:**

```text
   ------------------------------------------------  stick, 15 cm beyond ball line
                  |
        T•  ─0.3 m─▶    ─────────── 1.7 m ───────────▶  O
                  |   gate 6.0 cm
        |←──────────────── 2.0 m ─────────────────→|
```

### W2 · Stroke & Speed Primer (8 min)

**Equipment:** 1 putter · 3 balls · 8 tees.

**Setup:**

- Head gate: flat 1.2 m putt; one tee 5 mm outside the heel and one 5 mm outside the toe of the putter, level with the ball.
- Stroke-length markers on an open line with 12 m clear: tees 10 cm outside the toe line at 25 cm behind and 25 cm in front of the ball.
- Two lag targets: a hole (or tee) at 10 m and at 15 m.

**Procedure:**

1. Minutes 0–3 — head gate: 10 putts trail hand only, then 10 with both hands. The putter must pass the tees without touching.
2. Minutes 3–5 — rhythm: 6 putts with the 25 cm back / 25 cm through stroke, no target. Same rhythm every time; notice how far the ball rolls on today's green.
3. Minutes 5–8 — speed read: 3 balls to the 10 m target, 3 balls to the 15 m target, one uphill and one downhill if possible. Finish with one 1 m putt holed.

**Diagram:**

```text
   Head gate (1.2 m)           Rhythm (no target)               Lag targets
   |[═ putter ═]|              T        •        T              • ── 10 m ──▶ O
   |     •      | ──▶ O       25 cm   ball   25 cm  ──▶ roll      • ── 15 m ──▶ O
```

## Games — Start line & strike

### G1 · Gate Master

**Skill:** Start line · **Time:** 8 min · **Score:** points, 0–40, higher is better · **Slope class:** Flat

**Equipment:** 1 putter · 10 balls · 3 tees · tape measure.

**Setup:**

- Straight putt of 3.0 m. Tee at the ball position.
- Gate: 2 tees 0.3 m in front of the ball, **5.0 cm inner gap**, centred on the line to the hole centre. Check by rolling a ball by hand from the ball tee to the hole: it must pass the gate centre.

**Rules:**

1. Putt 20 balls (2 × 10), full routine on each.
2. Each ball: through the gate without touching a tee = 1 point; holed = +1 point.

**Scoring:** gate passes + holes. Tiebreak: more holes.

**App input:** gate passes (0–20), holes (0–20).

**Targets:** Beginner / mid ≥ 18 · Low single digit ≥ 28 · Tour ≥ 32

**Hard mode:** gate moved to 0.5 m (same 5.0 cm gap).

```text
                |
   T• ─0.3 m─▶     ─────────────── 2.7 m ───────────────▶  O
                |   gate 5.0 cm inner gap
   |←────────────────────── 3.0 m ──────────────────────→|
```

### G2 · Sweet Spot

**Skill:** Strike · **Time:** 8 min · **Score:** points, 0–20, higher is better · **Slope class:** Flat

**Equipment:** 1 putter · 10 balls · 1 dry-erase (whiteboard) marker · 1 cloth · 1 small mm ruler · 2 tees.

**Setup:**

- Putt of 9.0 m to a target tee.
- Sweet spot: hang the putter loosely by the grip and tap the face with a tee; where the head does not twist is the sweet spot. Mark it with a thin line on the top line.
- A 3 mm dot on the back of each ball, facing the putter at address.

**Rules:**

1. Putt one ball to the target tee.
2. Read where the dot transferred on the face; measure its heel–toe distance from the sweet-spot line.
3. Wipe the face. 10 balls.

**Scoring:** within ±3 mm = 2 points; ±3.1–6 mm = 1 point; beyond 6 mm = 0. Tiebreak: total ball distance from the target tee (lower wins).

**App input:** offset per ball in mm with H (heel) or T (toe) — the app computes points and shows heel/toe bias.

**Targets:** Beginner / mid ≥ 8 · Low single digit ≥ 13 · Tour ≥ 17

**Hard mode:** 15 m.

```text
   Face:   heel [ · · · ·|▒|█|▒|· · · · ] toe    █ = ±3 mm (2 pts)  ▒ = ±6 mm (1 pt)

   Green:  • ─────────────────────── 9.0 m ───────────────────────▶ T
```

## Games — Short putts & pressure

### G3 · Circle Streak

**Skill:** Short putts · **Time:** 15 min · **Score:** best streak, 0–100, higher is better · **Slope class:** Breaking (1–2%)

Based on Phil Mickelson's 3-ft circle drill: 10 tees round the hole, 100 in a row, restart on a miss ([GOLF.com](https://golf.com/instruction/phil-mickelson-key-3-footer-play-smart/)).

**Equipment:** 1 putter · 10 balls · 10 tees · timer.

**Setup:**

- Hole on a 1–2% slope.
- 10 tees 0.9 m from the hole centre, evenly spaced (≈ 0.57 m apart).

**Rules:**

1. Putt round the circle, one ball per tee, full routine. Reload and continue.
2. A miss resets the streak to 0; continue from the next tee.
3. Game ends at 100 in a row or after 15 minutes.

**Scoring:** best streak. Tiebreak: time to reach it (faster wins).

**App input:** best streak (0–100); time if 100 reached.

**Targets:** Beginner / mid ≥ 25 · Low single digit ≥ 50 · Tour 100 (Tour makes 99.4% from 0.9 m)

**Hard mode:** 1.2 m circle.

```text
                 T     T
             T             T
            T       O       T      10 tees, 0.9 m from hole centre
             T             T
                 T     T
```

### G4 · Step-Back Survival

**Skill:** Pressure · **Time:** ≈ 8 min · **Score:** farthest putt holed (m), 0–6.0, higher is better · **Slope class:** Flat (straight, may be gently uphill)

**Equipment:** 1 putter · 1 ball · 11 tees · tape measure.

**Setup:**

- One straight line to a hole. Tees at 1.0, 1.5, 2.0 … 6.0 m from the hole centre (every 0.5 m).

**Rules:**

1. Start at 1.0 m. Hole it → move back to the next tee.
2. Miss → lose a life and retry the same distance. You have 3 lives.
3. Game ends on the third miss or when 6.0 m is holed.

**Scoring:** farthest distance holed (0 if none). Tiebreak: lives left.

**App input:** farthest distance holed, lives left.

**Targets:** Beginner / mid ≥ 2.0 m · Low single digit ≥ 3.0 m · Tour ≥ 4.0 m

**Hard mode:** Breaking class (1–2% side slope); 2 lives.

```text
   6.0  5.5  5.0  4.5  4.0  3.5  3.0  2.5  2.0  1.5  1.0 m
    T    T    T    T    T    T    T    T    T    T    T  ─────▶  O
                                         start here ──┘  (make = step back, miss = −1 life)
```

### G5 · Short-Putt Strokes-Gained Test

**Skill:** Short putts · **Time:** 8 min · **Score:** strokes gained vs Tour, −7.1 to +2.9, higher is better · **Slope class:** Breaking (1–2%)

**Equipment:** 1 putter · 1 ball · 10 tees · tape measure.

**Setup:**

- Hole on a 1–2% slope.
- 10 tees: 2 each at 1.2, 1.5, 1.8, 2.1 and 2.4 m. Each pair has one left-to-right and one right-to-left putt.

**Rules:**

1. One ball, full routine, one attempt per tee, in any order.
2. Record make or miss per tee.

**Scoring:** makes − 7.1. The 7.1 is the Tour expected makes for this set (2 × (91.4% + 80.7% + 70.2% + 60.6% + 52.9%)). Example: 6 makes = −1.1.

**App input:** make/miss per tee (the app also builds make % by distance over time).

**Targets:** Beginner / mid ≥ −2.6 (4.5 makes) · Low single digit ≥ −1.1 (6 makes) · Tour ≥ 0.0

**Hard mode:** Steep class hole (2.5–3.5%).

```text
              2.4 T                      T 2.4
                 2.1 T                T 2.1
                    1.8 T          T 1.8        left column: breaks L→R
                       1.5 T    T 1.5           right column: breaks R→L
                          1.2 T T 1.2
                              O
```

## Games — Speed control

### G6 · Never Short

**Skill:** Speed · **Time:** 6 min · **Score:** points, 0–30, higher is better · **Slope class:** Flat

Based on Dave Pelz's finding that the best make speed rolls a missed putt about 43 cm (17 in) past the hole ([GOLF.com](https://golf.com/instruction/dave-pelzs-10-truths-about-putting/)).

**Equipment:** 1 putter · 10 balls · 4 tees · tape measure.

**Setup:**

- Putt of 6.1 m to a hole.
- Safe zone behind the hole: from the hole to 0.9 m past it, 0.3 m either side of the line. Mark the back corners with 2 tees and the 0.43 m "perfect" point with 1 tee.

**Rules:**

1. 10 balls, full routine each.
2. Holed = 3 points · stopped in the safe zone = 2 points · short of the hole or outside the zone = 0.

**Scoring:** total points. Tiebreak: more holed.

**App input:** holed (0–10), in zone (0–10).

**Targets:** Beginner / mid ≥ 10 · Low single digit ≥ 15 · Tour ≥ 20

**Hard mode:** zone 0.6 m deep; or downhill direction.

```text
                                                  T ┐
   • ─────────────────── 6.1 m ───────────────▶  O  T 0.43 m   safe zone: 0–0.9 m past,
                                                  T ┘ 0.9 m    0.6 m wide
```

### G7 · Leapfrog

**Skill:** Distance increments · **Time:** 8 min · **Score:** balls placed, 0–18, higher is better · **Slope class:** Flat

**Equipment:** 1 putter · 6 balls · 3 tees.

**Setup:**

- A straight stretch with 10 m clear. Tee at the start, tee at 3.0 m, tee at 9.0 m (limit).

**Rules:**

1. Ball 1 must stop past the 3.0 m tee and within 0.9 m of it.
2. Each next ball must stop past the previous ball and no more than 0.9 m beyond it. Putt on a line about 10 cm to the side to avoid hitting balls.
3. A ball short of the previous one, more than 0.9 m past it, or beyond 9.0 m ends the round.
4. 3 rounds; turn round and play the opposite direction in round 2.

**Scoring:** total balls placed over 3 rounds (max 6 per round). Tiebreak: higher single-round best.

**App input:** balls placed per round.

**Targets:** Beginner / mid ≥ 9 · Low single digit ≥ 15 · Tour 18

**Hard mode:** max gap 0.45 m.

```text
   T start                 T 3.0 m                                T 9.0 m limit
   • ────────────────────▶ |  ①  ②  ③  ④  ⑤  ⑥   ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ |
                              each ≤ 0.9 m past the previous ball
```

### G8 · Distance Call

**Skill:** Speed on demand · **Time:** 10 min · **Score:** points, 0–36, higher is better · **Slope class:** Flat

**Equipment:** 1 putter · 3 balls · 7 tees · tape measure · the app (or one die).

**Setup:**

- One straight line with 14 m clear. Start tee, then target tees at 3, 5, 7, 9, 11 and 13 m.

**Rules:**

1. Before each ball the app calls a random target (without the app, roll a die: 1 = 3 m, 2 = 5 m … 6 = 13 m).
2. Putt to stop at the called tee. Measure the ball's distance from that tee.
3. 12 balls.

**Scoring:** within 5% of the called distance = 3 points · within 10% = 2 · within 15% = 1 · beyond = 0. Example at 9 m: 0.45 m / 0.9 m / 1.35 m. Tiebreak: lower total error in metres.

**App input:** called distance and finish error (m) per ball — the app computes points.

**Targets:** Beginner / mid ≥ 12 · Low single digit ≥ 20 · Tour ≥ 27

**Hard mode:** targets move to 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, 19 m.

```text
   start    3 m     5 m     7 m     9 m     11 m    13 m
     T •──▶  T       T       T       T       T       T
            app calls one target per ball (random)
```

### G9 · Up & Down

**Skill:** Speed on slopes · **Time:** 10 min · **Score:** points, 0–24, higher is better · **Slope class:** Fall line, 2–3% gradient

**Equipment:** 1 putter · 2 balls · 6 tees · tape measure · optional phone level.

**Setup:**

- A hole on a straight fall line with a 2–3% gradient.
- Station U 4.5 m directly below the hole (uphill putt); station D 4.5 m directly above it (downhill putt).
- Zones: 0–0.6 m past the hole, 0.3 m either side of the line, on both sides of the hole.

**Rules:**

1. Putt 1 ball uphill from U, then walk round and putt 1 ball downhill from D.
2. 6 pairs (12 putts), always alternating.

**Scoring:** holed = 2 points · in zone = 1 · short or beyond = 0. Tiebreak: more downhill points.

**App input:** per putt: holed / zone / miss, and direction (up/down).

**Targets:** Beginner / mid ≥ 8 · Low single digit ≥ 13 · Tour ≥ 17

**Hard mode:** stations at 6.0 m.

```text
          D  •   4.5 m above (putt ↓)
             T   downhill zone limit (0.6 m below hole)   HIGH ↑
             O
             T   uphill zone limit (0.6 m above hole)     LOW ↓
          U  •   4.5 m below (putt ↑)
```

### G10 · Blind Feel

**Skill:** Feel / internal feedback · **Time:** 10 min · **Score:** points, 0–40, higher is better · **Slope class:** Flat

**Equipment:** 1 putter · 10 balls · 7 tees.

**Setup:**

- One straight line: start tee, target tees at 5.0 m and 10.0 m.
- Zone tees 0.5 m short and 0.5 m long of each target.

**Rules:**

1. Alternate targets: 5 m, 10 m, 5 m … (20 balls).
2. Aim with eyes open, close them before the backstroke, keep them closed until you call "short", "good" or "long" out loud.
3. Then look.

**Scoring:** correct call = 1 point · ball finishes in the ±0.5 m zone = 1 point. Tiebreak: more correct calls.

**App input:** per ball: call and result (short/good/long) — the app scores both.

**Targets:** Beginner / mid ≥ 18 · Low single digit ≥ 25 · Tour ≥ 31

**Hard mode:** zone ±0.3 m.

```text
                     0.5 T 0.5                        0.5 T 0.5
   T • ──────────── |  5 m  | ────────────────────── | 10 m  |
                  short good long                   short good long
```

## Games — Lag putting

### G11 · Lag Darts

**Skill:** Lag proximity · **Time:** 10 min · **Score:** points, 0–45, higher is better · **Slope class:** Mixed — station 1 uphill, station 2 downhill, station 3 sidehill

**Equipment:** 1 putter · 3 balls · 3 station tees · 12 coins or tees for the rings · tape measure.

**Setup:**

- Stations at 8, 12 and 16 m from one hole (uphill, downhill, sidehill).
- Three rings round the hole centre, each marked at 4 points (12, 3, 6, 9 o'clock): 0.5 m, 1.0 m and 2.0 m radius.

**Rules:**

1. 3 balls from each station, full routine each (9 balls).
2. Score each ball where it stops. Measure with the tape when a ball sits near a ring.

**Scoring:** holed = 5 · inside 0.5 m = 3 · inside 1.0 m = 2 · inside 2.0 m = 1 · outside = 0. Tiebreak: more balls inside 0.5 m.

**App input:** per ball: ring hit (holed / 0.5 / 1.0 / 2.0 / out), or the leave in metres.

**Targets:** Beginner / mid ≥ 10 · Low single digit ≥ 16 · Tour ≥ 22 (Tour average lag leave ≈ 0.7 m)

**Hard mode:** stations at 12, 16 and 20 m.

```text
                         ( 2.0 m ring )
   T 16 m (sidehill)    ( ( 1.0 m ) )
   T 12 m (downhill)   ( ( ( 0.5 ) ) )
   T  8 m (uphill)  ─▶ ( ( (  O  ) ) )      holed 5 · 0.5 m 3 · 1.0 m 2 · 2.0 m 1
```

### G12 · Lag Ladder

**Skill:** Lag under pressure · **Time:** 10–20 min · **Score:** balls used, 9–75, lower is better · **Slope class:** Any (log it)

Based on Phil Mickelson's lag drill from 30, 40 and 50 ft, as described by Butch Harmon ([Golf Digest](https://www.golfdigest.com/story/harmon_gd0805)). The 3-in-a-row standard here is the documented short version ([Golf Digest](https://www.golfdigest.com/story/3-tour-secrets-thatll-get-you-making-putts)).

**Equipment:** 1 putter · 3 balls · 3 station tees · 4 coins or tees · tape measure.

**Setup:**

- Stations at 9, 12 and 15 m from one hole.
- Circle of 0.9 m radius round the hole centre, marked at 4 points.

**Rules:**

1. Start at 12 m. All 3 balls in a row must finish inside the circle (or holed). Any miss → start that station's set again.
2. Then 9 m, then 15 m.
3. Count every ball putted. Stop at 75 balls (score = 75, "not cleared").

**Scoring:** total balls used to clear all three stations (best possible 9). Tiebreak: fewer balls at 15 m.

**App input:** balls used per station (or a tap per ball: in / out).

**Targets:** Beginner / mid ≤ 50 · Low single digit ≤ 30 · Tour ≤ 16

**Hard mode:** 5 in a row per station (Mickelson's full version).

```text
   T 15 m            T 12 m             T 9 m                        ×
     • ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ • ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ • ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ▶ ×  O  ×
                                                                     ×
   order: 12 → 9 → 15 m        × = circle marks, 0.9 m from hole centre
```

## Games — Green reading & scoring

### G13 · Slope Sense

**Skill:** Reading (slope feel) · **Time:** 8 min · **Score:** points, 0–24, higher is better · **Slope class:** 8 spots spanning 0.5–4%

Based on AimPoint Express (Mark Sweeney): Tour players call a slope % with their feet while a caddie checks a digital level ([USA Today via Yahoo](https://ca.sports.yahoo.com/news/aimpoint-express-why-many-pga-212108528.html)).

**Equipment:** 1 digital level (≥ 40 cm), or a phone level app on a straight 40 cm board · 8 tees.

**Setup:**

- 8 spots across the green with side slopes between 0.5% and 4%, each marked with a tee. Pick an imaginary putt line at each (e.g. toward the nearest hole).

**Rules:**

1. Stand on the line, feet shoulder-width either side of it, facing along it. Close your eyes for 3–5 seconds.
2. Call the side slope to the nearest 0.5% and the low side ("2.0, falls left").
3. Lay the level across the line, centred on the tee, and record the true value.

**Scoring:** wrong low side = 0 · within ±0.25% = 3 · within ±0.5% = 2 · within ±1.0% = 1 · beyond = 0. Tiebreak: lower total error in %.

**App input:** per spot: called % and side, measured % and side — the app scores and tracks bias (over/under-feeling).

**Targets:** Beginner / mid ≥ 10 · Low single digit ≥ 16 · Tour ≥ 20

**Hard mode:** read at the midpoint of real 6–10 m putts instead of chosen spots.

```text
        T1            T2              T3          (8 spots, 0.5–4% side slope)
                T4          T5
        T6              T7            T8                  O

   At each tee:   foot ▌  │ putt line  ▐ foot     level laid ACROSS the line
```

### G14 · Fall-Line Clock

**Skill:** Reading + short putts · **Time:** 10 min · **Score:** makes, 0–16, higher is better · **Slope class:** Hole on a 2–3% slope

**Equipment:** 1 putter · 1 ball · 8 tees · tape measure.

**Setup:**

- A hole on a 2–3% slope.
- Find the fall line: from 1.0 m below the hole, roll putts from slightly different spots until one goes in dead straight. That line is 6 o'clock (uphill); straight above the hole is 12 o'clock (downhill).
- 8 tees 1.5 m from the hole centre at 12, 1:30, 3, 4:30, 6, 7:30, 9 and 10:30.

**Rules:**

1. One putt per tee, no trial putts, full routine. Say your aim before putting.
2. Two laps (16 putts); start lap 2 at a different tee.
3. Note each miss as high side or low side.

**Scoring:** total makes. Tiebreak: fewer low-side misses.

**App input:** per tee: make / miss high / miss low — the app shows reading bias by clock position.

**Targets:** Beginner / mid ≥ 8 · Low single digit ≥ 11 · Tour ≥ 13 (Tour makes 80.7% from 1.5 m)

**Hard mode:** clock at 2.1 m.

```text
                      12 T          (12 = straight downhill)
             10:30 T          T 1:30
           9 T          O          T 3      all tees 1.5 m from hole centre
              7:30 T          T 4:30
                       6 T          (6 = straight uphill)
```

### G15 · Putting Round

**Skill:** Scoring (all skills) · **Time:** 20 min · **Score:** total putts, lower is better · **Slope class:** Mixed — at least 6 uphill, 6 downhill, 6 sidehill holes

**Equipment:** 1 putter · 1 ball · 18 tees (or 9 used twice) · tape measure.

**Setup:**

- 18 holes: 2 each at 0.9, 1.5, 2.1, 3.0, 4.5, 6.0, 9.0, 12.0 and 15.0 m, spread round 3–4 holes.
- Enter the card in the app first (hole, distance, slope), so the round can be replayed on the same green.

**Rules:**

1. Play holes 1–18 in order. One ball, full routine, hole out everything — no gimmes, no second tries.
2. Record putts per hole.

**Scoring:** total putts. Tour "par" ≈ 30: about 10.4 for the eight 0.9–3.0 m holes and 19.7 for the ten 4.5–15 m holes, from Tour make % and Mark Broadie's strokes-gained baseline. Tiebreak: fewer 3-putts.

**App input:** putts per hole — the app shows strokes gained vs Tour par per distance band and counts 3-putts.

**Targets:** Beginner / mid ≤ 36 · Low single digit ≤ 33 · Tour ≤ 30

**Hard mode:** replace both 0.9 m holes with 2.4 m holes (Tour par ≈ 30.9).

```text
   Card:  H1 0.9  H2 6.0  H3 2.1  H4 12.0  H5 1.5  H6 9.0  H7 3.0  H8 15.0  H9 4.5
          H10–18 = same distances, different holes and slopes

          T 15 m                 T 9 m
                  O A                     T 1.5 m
          T 2.1 m        T 4.5 m      O B
                  T 0.9 m                 T 12 m
          T 6 m          O C          T 3 m
```
